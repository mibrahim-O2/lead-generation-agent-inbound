# Example Solar Co - FAQ

Fictional demo content for a fictional company. Nothing here is a quote, a
promise or professional advice.

## Net metering process

Net metering lets a customer export surplus solar electricity to the grid. The
application goes to the local distribution company, which reviews it and makes
the decision. Our team prepares the paperwork and submits it on the customer's
behalf. We cannot say whether or when an application will be accepted, because
that decision is made by the distribution company.

## System sizing

The right system size depends on your recent electricity bills, your daytime
and night-time usage, the usable roof or ground space, and whether you want
battery backup. We size a system after reviewing a few months of bills and
doing a site survey. We do not recommend a size from a single message.

## Installation time

Installation time depends on the system size, the mounting structure and site
conditions. After the site survey our team shares a proposed schedule. We do
not commit to dates before the survey is done.

## Warranty

Panels, inverters and batteries carry their manufacturers' warranties, and the
terms differ by brand and model. Installation workmanship is covered
separately. The exact warranty terms for your equipment are listed in the
written proposal.

## Financing

Some customers pay through bank solar-financing schemes. Eligibility and terms
are decided by the bank, not by us. We can share the documents a bank usually
asks for, and our written proposal can be used in a financing application.

## Site survey

A site survey is a visit by our technical team to check the roof or ground
area, shading, the existing electrical connection and the best place for the
equipment. A written proposal is prepared after the survey.

## Batteries and backup

A hybrid system with batteries can keep selected loads running during a power
cut. How long depends on the battery capacity and what is switched on. Our team
discusses your backup needs before proposing a battery size.
